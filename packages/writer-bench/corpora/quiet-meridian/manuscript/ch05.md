# Chapter 5: The Clerk's Distance

<!-- ref: ch05:p001 -->
They followed the western tunnel after the storm passed. Vey remained at the Engine with the wardens, claiming the city required an operator. The black rail car rolled ahead without power, always one bend beyond the thermal lamp. Its wheels made no sound. Maintenance alcoves appeared at regular intervals, each containing a chair turned toward Ilyr and a cup fused to its saucer by frost. When Sera looked back, the eastern signal stayed green no matter how far they walked. The tunnel's bricks changed from city red to an older gray that swallowed the lamp rather than reflecting it. On the walls, distance markers counted upward in waking numbers and downward in the amber reflections cast by Sera's map film.

<!-- ref: ch05:p002 -->
Kellan carried water and a portable sleep coil. He offered explanations in fragments: Tev had recruited him to monitor shared dreams; the counter-rhythm began as a test; Vey learned of it only after the first citywide pulse. Each fact arrived polished and isolated from responsibility. Sera asked when he had last spoken to Tev. Kellan answered by adjusting the coil's straps.

<!-- ref: ch05:p003 -->
At the fourth marker they found a maintenance room stocked with recent food, charged lamps, and paper schedules. Someone had folded the schedules into birds but left their springs unwound. A ledger recorded deliveries from the Registry under Kellan's authorization code. The entries continued through the previous week. Sera placed the ledger beside his water flask and waited.

<!-- ref: ch05:p004 -->
Kellan said he had supplied the room without knowing who collected the stores. Packages disappeared when he left them during artificial night. Replies arrived as alterations to his own sleep maps: a stair moved, a door changed color, a childhood street acquired a western exit. He believed Tev was answering because the changes referred to places only the three of them had known.

<!-- ref: ch05:p005 -->
Sera asked why he had never shown her. Kellan said Tev's first message ordered him to keep Sera away until the Meridian Engine could distinguish chosen routes from imposed ones. Later messages became less personal and more precise. Kellan could not tell whether caution still belonged to Tev or to a machine using Tev's memories. He had decided uncertainty excused obedience.

<!-- ref: ch05:p006 -->
His deeper betrayal emerged only when Sera found Tev's original wall chart rolled inside the portable coil. Kellan had taken it before the Registry sealed the apartment. He gave Vey a copy with the Wake Station removed, then delayed every order that might bring Sera home. He believed he was protecting her from the Engine and Tev from Vey. In practice, he had protected his authority to decide for them both.

<!-- ref: ch05:p007 -->
Sera understood why he had done it, which made forgiveness less available rather than more. She took the wall chart and returned his water. "You may walk beside me," she said. "That is not the same as being trusted." Kellan accepted the distinction without asking how long it would last. Some distances were honest only when nobody called them bridges.

<!-- ref: ch05:p008 -->
The portable coil woke in Kellan's hands. Amber lines climbed his sleeves and projected a map of the tunnel ahead. One route ended at the Wake Station. Another curved back to Ilyr and erased the station behind it. A third followed no physical tunnel at all, crossing blank stone toward a point labeled SERA NAHL: ARRIVAL RECORDED. Sera checked the numerals twice, then held the film away in case her touch was influencing it. The date beside the impossible arrival remained twenty-two years before her birth.
