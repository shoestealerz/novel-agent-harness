# Chapter 2: The Sleep Registry

<!-- ref: ch02:p001 -->
The Registry occupied an old station whose platforms had been bricked over when the city abandoned the Ash Rail. Glass map tables filled the concourse where passengers once queued. Beneath each table, coils translated sleeping citizens' remembered motion into lines of amber light. Clerks worked in slippers to keep footsteps from contaminating the motion sensors. The ceiling still announced trains in letters reversed by decades of repainting, and brass benches bore the shallow dents of luggage belonging to travelers now old or dead. Even sealed, the building behaved as though departure were merely delayed. Sera fed Tev's scratched numbers into her terminal. Instead of opening a route, they selected five anonymous dreams recorded after his disappearance.

<!-- ref: ch02:p002 -->
Each dream showed the same impossible corridor running beneath Ilyr from heat to ice. Doors appeared where known tunnels ended, and every dreamer heard a slow pulse through the floor. Registry manuals called shared architecture a convergence error: unrelated memories forced into the same shape by damaged coils. Tev's notes proposed the opposite. The machine did not merely record routes, he wrote. Under certain loads, it suggested them.

<!-- ref: ch02:p003 -->
Director Vey arrived before Sera could copy the files. She wore mirrored lenses that reflected the map tables as twin burning cities. Vey said Tev had stolen a brass calibration token capable of unlocking the sealed rail controls. If Sera found it, she must surrender it before the coming thermal surge. Sera denied possessing the token. This was true, though Kellan's attention moved at once toward the pocket where she had hidden Tev's film.

<!-- ref: ch02:p004 -->
After Vey left, Sera found the brass token taped beneath Tev's former desk. One face bore the Registry compass; the other had been filed smooth except for a crescent-shaped nick. She placed it in the lower map-table drawer, locked the drawer with Kellan's registry key, and returned the key to him. "Leave one door closed," she said. Kellan slipped the key onto his neck cord. They went upstairs without reopening the drawer.

<!-- ref: ch02:p005 -->
Kellan brought tea to the projection room and pretended not to watch the copied dreams. When Sera mentioned the pulse beneath the corridor, his cup stopped halfway to his mouth. He admitted that the Registry had detected the same rhythm in tram-power fluctuations: three quick drops, a pause, then two. Vey had ordered the fluctuations classified as maintenance noise. Kellan had obeyed because disobedience, he said, was easier to admire before it cost someone heat.

<!-- ref: ch02:p006 -->
The five dream records carried maintenance signatures. Four belonged to sleeping citizens and one to a technician identified only as T.N. That technician had recalibrated the corridor model eleven times since Tev vanished. The latest signature was six days old. Sera compared the slant of the N with Tev's scratched film. Both ended in a downward hook caused by the stiffness in his injured right thumb.

<!-- ref: ch02:p007 -->
Kellan warned that handwriting habits could be copied and technician initials reassigned. The Registry allowed supervisors to sign for absent workers, though Vey claimed no one had used that exception. More troubling was the timing: every T.N. calibration preceded a citywide three-and-two power fluctuation by exactly nine minutes. Whoever signed the records either predicted the pulse or caused it.

<!-- ref: ch02:p008 -->
The signatures did not prove Tev lived. A stolen habit was still easier to believe than a dead man maintaining machinery. Yet the empty suit, the missing wall map, and the fresh calibrations no longer resembled separate accidents. Sera copied the records onto map film and hid them inside the paper bird. She left the original files open so the audit log would show that someone had looked rather than allow Vey to call the discovery a system error. Outside the Registry, the fixed sunlight dimmed from white to rust as a thermal storm gathered beyond the eastern shields.
